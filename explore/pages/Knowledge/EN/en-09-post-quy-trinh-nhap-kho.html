From Barcodes to Receiving: Fulfillment at SpeeGo

SpeeGo Editorial Team · Updated September 17, 2026
Share Facebook ↗ LinkedIn ↗ Copy link

Explore the four steps that connect products, inventory, and orders in a single workflow.

Barcode setup

Create or assign a barcode for each product. SpeeGo provides barcode files on request.

Create an inbound request

Select your SKUs and quantities. The OMS confirms the request and returns an IR code; print it and attach it to your packages before shipping.

Receiving and inspection

Shipments are inspected before storage. Inventory is updated in the OMS for sellers to monitor.

Fulfillment and shipping

Orders are packed with video documentation and shipped through USPS, UPS, or FedEx, depending on requirements.
