ARTICLE CONTENTS

Cargo details

Prepare product names, quantities, weights, and package dimensions. Consistent information helps the team understand your shipment requirements.

Origin, destination, and timing

Identify the pickup address, delivery location, and preferred timeline. Discuss any special requirements before choosing a shipping method.

Choosing a shipping method

SpeeGo supports full container loads (FCL) and less-than-container loads (LCL), along with ocean, air, and road freight options suited to your shipment.

Confirm before shipping

Work with SpeeGo to confirm the quote, estimated schedule, documentation, and main contact before handing over your cargo.
