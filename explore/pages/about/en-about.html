OUR JOURNEY

Our history

Founded in 2015, SpeeGo Logistics began with a simple mission: make international business simpler for companies, store owners, and entrepreneurs — removing barriers in manufacturing and logistics.

After nearly a decade, we have built a network of certified manufacturing partners and 5,000–10,000 m² transit warehouses in the US, Canada, and Australia, serving more than 5,000 customers worldwide.
