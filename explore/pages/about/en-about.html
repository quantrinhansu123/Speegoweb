OUR JOURNEY

Our history

Founded in 2015, SpeeGo Logistics began with a simple mission: make international business simpler for companies, store owners, and entrepreneurs — removing barriers in manufacturing and logistics.

After nearly a decade, we have built a network of certified manufacturing partners and 5,000–10,000 m² transit warehouses in the US, Canada, and Australia, serving more than 5,000 customers worldwide.

In an era where speed and connection shape every value, SpeeGo was born to build a global manufacturing and logistics ecosystem — where ideas become reality faster, more sustainably, and more intelligently.

As we expanded, Ceva Nails Supply became SpeeGo’s specialized arm in the Nails industry: wholesale supply, ordering, and custom manufacturing of products, accessories, equipment, and packaging for the sector.
