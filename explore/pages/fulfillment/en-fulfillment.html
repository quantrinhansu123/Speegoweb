FULFILLMENT WORKFLOW

Five steps. One US-standard workflow.

01

Barcode setup

Create or assign a barcode for each product. Barcode PDF files provided on request.

02

Inbound request (IR)

Create an inbound request in the OMS: pick SKUs and quantities. The OMS confirms and returns an IR code to print and attach to each package.

03

Receiving, inspection & put-away

Every shipment is inspected before put-away. Inventory updates automatically in the OMS in real time.

04

Fulfillment & shipping

Orders are picked, packed with video records, and shipped via USPS, UPS, or FedEx.

05

Returns handling

SpeeGo handles the full returns intake and processing workflow, free of charge on every order.

SERVICE POLICY

Fulfillment policy

Each case below is assessed from the order record, valid documentation, and the reconciliation between the customer, the SpeeGo warehouse, and the carrier.

01 Goods lost in the warehouse +

Eligibility: Goods were received into inventory correctly but were lost while in storage.

Compensation: If an entire lot is lost, SpeeGo reimburses 100% of the merchandise cost and shipping fees when the customer provides complete valid documentation.

02 Incorrect packing by the warehouse +

Warehouse error: Staff packed the wrong SKU. SpeeGo covers the cost of reshipping to the buyer and reimburses 100% of merchandise cost, up to $20/item.

Customer data error: If the wrong SKU results from the customer printing or applying an incorrect product barcode, SpeeGo has no compensation obligation.

03 Wrong label or delivery address +

Failed delivery: If the address is missing or the warehouse entered incorrect information / printed or applied the wrong label, SpeeGo covers the cost of reshipping the order.

Wrong address delivery: If the error originated in the warehouse, SpeeGo reships free of charge and reimburses 100% of merchandise cost, up to $3/item.

Customer cancels before reship: Fulfillment and shipping fees for that order are refunded.

Note: If the seller provided inaccurate information, SpeeGo has no compensation obligation.

04 Damage during packing or domestic transit +

Warehouse packing error: If a packing error causes a refund, SpeeGo refunds 100% of fulfillment and shipping fees and supports reimbursement of 100% of merchandise cost, up to $20/order.

Damage in transit: If the warehouse packed as agreed, SpeeGo helps the customer reconcile with USPS, UPS, or FedEx to file a claim.

Exclusion: No compensation when the customer requested minimal packing to reduce cost and accepted the risk.

05 Marked “Delivered” but not received +

Eligibility: Applies to orders that require a signature. The customer must help gather information from the buyer and support the reconciliation process.

Handling: SpeeGo reconciles with USPS, UPS, or FedEx. If the carrier confirms loss, the claim follows the carrier’s policy.

Exclusion: If status remains “Delivered” and the carrier does not confirm loss, SpeeGo has no compensation obligation.

06 Orders shipped later than the SLA +

Eligibility: A fulfillment tracking number was created but the order was not shipped within 3 business days, excluding U.S. weekends and holidays.

Handling: If warehouse processing exceeds the SLA by more than 3 business days, SpeeGo refunds 100% of the fulfillment fee for that order.

Exclusion: If the warehouse handed the order to USPS, UPS, or FedEx within SLA but the delay occurred in transit, SpeeGo is not liable for compensation.

FILING A CLAIM

Submission deadlines

  • Damage, incorrect rates, or issues with the goods: Within 3 days of delivery to the recipient.
